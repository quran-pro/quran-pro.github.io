Tasbih Counter के बारे में

Online Tasbih Counter क्या है?

Tasbih Counter ek digital zikr-ginti ka tool hai jo Quran Pro ne khaas taur par un musalmano ke liye banaya hai jo apna zikr, wazifa ya tasbih physical dane wali tasbih ke bina bhi asaani se count karna chahte hain. Chahe aap ghar par ho, safar mein ho ya office mein — bas ek tap se aap SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, Astaghfirullah, La ilaha illallah ya Durood Sharif jaise kalimat ka zikr shuru kar sakte hain aur is Online Tasbih Counter se apni ginti record kar sakte hain. Ye tool bilkul free hai aur ismein na koi app download karni padti hai, na koi sign-up ki zaroorat hai — bas browser kholiye aur zikr shuru kar dijiye.

Is Digital Tasbih Counter ko istemal karne ka tarika

Sabse pehle upar diye gaye "ज़िक्र चुनें" section se apna pasandeeda kalima chunein. Uske baad har baar zikr padhne par bade gol button par tap karein — har tap par counter automatically badh jayega aur ring progress bhi update hoti rahegi। Jab aapka round (jaise 33 ya 100) poora ho jata hai, to app khud-ba-khud round reset kar deta hai aur agar "Auto Mode" on hai to agla zikr apne aap shuru ho jata hai, jisse lagataar wazifa karna aur bhi asaan ho jata hai।

Daily Target aur Streak Tracker

Ye Tasbih Counter sirf ginti tak mehdood nahi hai — ismein aap apna roz ka target bhi set kar sakte hain, jaise "har din 100 baar zikr karna hai"। Jab bhi aap apna daily goal poora karte hain, app ek mubarakbaad ka message dikhata hai aur aapki "Streak" (lagataar din) ko bhi track karta hai। Isse aapko rozana zikr karne ki aadat banane mein madad milti hai aur aap apni ibadat ki pragati bhi dekh sakte hain।

Kyun istemal karein Digital Tasbih?

  • Bilkul free, koi download ya login zaroori nahi
  • Mobile aur computer, dono par kaam karta hai
  • Aapka data safely aapke browser mein save rehta hai
  • Daily goal, streak aur lifetime count track hote hain
  • Auto Mode se ek se zyada zikr lagataar padhna asaan
  • Simple, saaf aur distraction-free design

Zikr ki Fazilat (Importance)

Allah ke zikr ka bayaan Quran Sharif mein baar baar aaya hai। Zikr karne se dil ko sukoon milta hai aur insaan Allah ke qareeb hota hai। SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar aur Astaghfirullah jaise kalimaat ko roz padhna sunnat bhi hai aur inki bohot fazilat bhi bayaan ki gayi hai। Isi wajah se Quran Pro ne ye Online Tasbih Counter banaya hai, taaki har koi asaani se apna rozana ka zikr mukammal kar sake।

FAQs — Aksar Poochhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Online Tasbih Counter kya hai?

Ye ek digital tool hai jisse aap SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar jaise zikr ko mobile ya computer par gin sakte hain, bina physical tasbih ke।

Kya Tasbih Counter free hai?

Ji haan, Quran Pro ka Tasbih Counter bilkul free hai aur ismein koi login ya download ki zaroorat nahi hai।

Kya mera zikr count save rehta hai?

Ji haan, aapka count, streak aur daily progress automatically aapke browser mein save ho jata hai, taaki agli baar aane par bhi wahi se jaari rakh sakein।

Auto Mode kya karta hai?

Auto Mode on karne par ek zikr ka round poora hote hi agla zikr apne aap shuru ho jata hai, jisse lagataar wazifa karna aasan ho jata hai।
