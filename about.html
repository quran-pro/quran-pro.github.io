About Quran Pro

Quran Pro ek free website hai jo Quran Sharif ka mukammal Hindi aur Urdu tarjuma, tafseer, Namaz ka aasaan tarika, masnoon duayen aur digital tasbih counter ek hi jagah par provide karti hai — bina kisi charge ke, sabke liye.

Hamara Maqsad

Hamara maqsad hai ki har koi apni zabaan mein Quran Sharif ko aasani se samajh sake. Isi liye Quran Pro par Hindi aur Urdu dono tarjume diye gaye hain, saath hi Namaz ka mukammal guide bhi hai jisse naye padhne wale bhi seekh sakein.

Features

  • Quran Sharif - Hindi aur Urdu tarjuma
  • Surah-wise Tafseer
  • Namaz ka mukammal tarika aur niyat
  • Masnoon Duayen
  • Digital Tasbih Counter
  • Ayatul Kursi aur Surah Al-Mulk quick access

Developer

Quran Pro ko design aur develop kiya hai Farukh Cheeta ne, ek behtar aur asaan Quran-reading experience dene ke maqsad se.
